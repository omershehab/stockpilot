# Code of Conduct

StockPilot is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep discussions focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on a maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents the StockPilot community.
